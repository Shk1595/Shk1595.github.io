FAQ

How do I steer?

Swipe anywhere to point the head up, down, left or right. Prefer buttons? Turn on arrows in Settings > Controls.

What do the coloured rings around apples mean?

Corner brackets: a plain apple. Purple ring with gold corners: a twin apple, which flips you so your tail becomes your head. Orange and blue dashed rings: a portal pair. Eat one and you come out of the other.

How do I earn coins?

1 coin per apple, 10 per gold coin, and a free gift on the Home screen every 6 hours. Spend them in the Store on new snakes, each with its own world. Four premium sets are available as purchases.

I bought something but it's missing.

Open Store or Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I turn off ads?

Buy Remove Ads in the Store or Settings. It's a one-time purchase and restores on new devices.

My rank doesn't show.

Sign in to Game Center (iOS Settings > Game Center), then finish a game. Ranks can take a few seconds to update.

How do I change ad privacy choices?

Tracking: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the EEA/UK: the Ad privacy choices link at the bottom of Coil's Settings. See the privacy policy.
