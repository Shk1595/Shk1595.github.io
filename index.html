WHAT OUR GAMES COST

Every Mindgrid game costs $0 to download. They are paid for in two ways, and you choose how much you spend:

  • Ads, shown by Google AdMob. Coil plays a short ad between some games, never in the middle of a run. 2048 Steps only shows an ad when you choose to watch one for extra plays.
  • Optional one-time purchases. Things like Remove Ads or extra content. There are no subscriptions, no loot boxes and nothing you have to buy to finish or enjoy a game.

Purchases are made through Apple, are tied to your Apple ID and can be restored on a new iPhone from the game's Settings. The App Store always shows the price in your local currency before you confirm. Prices on this site are the US price and may differ slightly by country.
