FAQ

How do I get more plays?

Watch an optional reward ad when you run out, or buy Unlimited Play once to play without limits. Plays are only added after the ad has finished.

I bought Unlimited Play but it's missing.

Use the restore option in the game while signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Can I play offline?

Yes, with the plays you have or with Unlimited Play. Ads, Game Center and purchases need an internet connection.

My score isn't on the leaderboard.

Sign in to Game Center (iOS Settings > Game Center), then finish a game.

How do I change ad privacy choices?

Where required in your region, use the ad privacy option in the game's Settings. See the privacy policy.
